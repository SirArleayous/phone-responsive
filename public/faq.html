Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working and advertising on SINEX.

How fast do I get paid for completing tasks?

Task submissions are reviewed within 1 to 24 hours. Once verified, your funds are credited directly to your SINEX wallet for instant bank withdrawal.

What is the minimum withdrawal limit?

The minimum payout balance threshold is ₦1,000. You can request payouts directly to any registered bank account in Nigeria.

Can I advertise my business on SINEX?

Yes! Switch to a Client account or register as a brand to fund your wallet and create targeted WhatsApp, download, or review campaigns.
